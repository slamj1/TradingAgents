# NVDA Technical Analysis Report (as of 2026-05-04)

## Executive Summary

NVDA has experienced a **powerful V-shaped recovery** from its March 27, 2026 low of ~$167.52 to its April 27, 2026 peak of ~$216.83—a remarkable **~29.4% rally in just four weeks**. However, the stock has since entered a **sharp correction**, falling ~8.5% from the April 27 high to $198.45 on May 1, 2026—the last available trading day. The technical indicators present a mixed picture: the medium-term trend remains bullish with the price still well above the 50-day Simple Moving Average, but short-term momentum has decisively shifted bearish with the MACD histogram turning negative and RSI dropping from overbought to neutral territory.

## 1. Price Action and Trend Analysis

### The Four-Month Decline (Nov 2025 – March 2026)
NVDA experienced a sustained downtrend from its November 4 starting point of ~$198.67 to its March 27 low of $167.52. Key phases:
- **November slump**: From ~$198 on Nov 4 to ~$172.91 low on Nov 21, driven on heavy volume (343M+ shares on Nov 20, 320M+ on Nov 25)
- **December chopping**: Oscillated between $170–$190 without clear direction; the December 26 high of $190.52 represented a modest relief rally
- **January consolidation**: Continued ranging between $178–$192 with no decisive breakout
- **February selloff**: Dramatic drop from $194.44 to a February 5 low of $171.02 on 206M+ shares
- **March double-bottom attempt**: Bounced briefly to ~$183 in early March, but collapsed to the ultimate low of $167.52 on March 27 on 196M shares

### The April Rally (March 27 – April 27)
The recovery was explosive and broad-based:
- March 31: Strong bullish candle from $166.96 to $174.40 on 226M shares
- April 6–13: Gradual build from ~$177 to ~$189 on steady volume
- April 14–27: **Momentum parabolic phase** — from $190.84 to $216.83 on volume averaging 150M–214M shares. The April 27 move to $216.83 on 187M shares was a definitive breakout above prior resistance zones

### The May Pullback (April 27 – May 1)
- April 27 high: $216.83
- April 28: Opened lower at $209.49, closed $213.17 (gap down)
- April 29: Continued weakness, closed $209.25
- April 30: Broke below $200, closed **$199.57** on heavy 225M shares — the highest volume day in this pullback
- May 1: Closed **$198.45**, continuing the decline

**Key insight**: The pullback has retraced only ~$18.40 of the $49.31 rally. This is a shallow correction relative to the move's height, which could signal a healthy pullback within a bull trend—if support holds.

The **rising ATR** confirms that volatility is increasing during this pullback phase. Elevated ATR means larger price swings and wider stop-loss distances may be warranted. It also signals that the market is in a **discovery/gasping phase** where uncertainty is elevated.

### Volume-Weighted Moving Average (VWMA)

When price trades below the VWMA, it indicates that **recent selling has been accompanied by above-average volume**, adding weight to the bears. This reinforces the bearish short-term bias. The VWMA acts as dynamic resistance at ~$203; any bounce attempt should target this level.
