# RRC (Range Resources Corporation) — Technical Analysis Report
**As of May 7, 2026**

## Executive Summary

RRC has experienced a dynamic price journey over the past three months: a powerful rally from ~$36.50 (early February) to a peak of $48.31 (March 27), followed by a correction of approximately 14.7% down to $41.17 (May 6). The stock is currently in a **post-rally consolidation phase**, trading below its 50-day SMA but well above its 200-day SMA. This suggests a **healthy pullback within a longer-term uptrend**, though short-term momentum is bearish. Key indicators point to caution in the near term with potential for a rebound as the price approaches oversold levels.

## Detailed Technical Analysis

### 1. Trend Analysis — Moving Averages

**50-Day SMA: $42.90**
The most recent price of $41.17 sits **$1.73 (4.0%) below** the 50-day SMA. The 50-SMA itself has been steadily declining from $44.34 (April 7) to $42.90 (May 6), confirming weakening near-term momentum. This bearish alignment suggests that sellers have dominated the April–May period.

**200-Day SMA: $37.76**
Despite the recent pullback, RRC remains **$3.41 (9.0%) above** its 200-day SMA, which has been on a slow but steady upward trajectory from $37.40 to $37.76. This is a critical bullish signal — the long-term trend remains intact. A golden cross occurred long ago when the 50-SMA crossed above the 200-SMA during the rally from February, and this relationship remains undisturbed.

**Trend Verdict:** Medium-term bearish, long-term bullish. The stock is in a classic "pullback-to-trend" scenario.

### 2. Momentum — RSI (Relative Strength Index)

**Current RSI: 41.63 (May 6)**

The RSI dropped sharply from 52.01 (May 5) to 41.63 (May 6), a 10.4-point plunge in a single session. This indicates accelerating selling pressure. However, the RSI is **not yet in oversold territory (< 30)**, suggesting there's still room for the price to decline further if bearish sentiment intensifies.

Looking back at the RSI trajectory:
- April 7: 55.13 (starting of the decline)
- April 14: 37.63 (near oversold)
- April 22: 53.31 (brief recovery spike)
- May 6: 41.63 (declined again)

This oscillation pattern reveals **indecision** — the stock lacked sustained bullish conviction during the mid-April bounce. The proximity to the 40-level makes it a watchpoint: a break below 35 would approach oversold conditions, potentially setting up a bounce opportunity.

### 3. Momentum — MACD

**MACD Line: -0.078 (May 6)**  
**MACD Histogram: -0.061 (May 6)**

The MACD line crossed below zero on May 6, transitioning from +0.057 on May 5. This is a **bearish signal** — momentum has shifted negative. Furthermore, this reversal followed a brief bullish blip just 2–3 days prior, which looks like a **failed bounce** rather than a genuine trend reversal.

The dramatic context:
- MACD peaked at +1.09 on April 7
- Dropped to -0.20 by April 22 (peak selling)
- Recovered to near zero by April 30
- Now turned negative again at -0.078

This pattern suggests the **bearish momentum from March–April has persisted**, with temporary relief rallies failing to push momentum into positive territory.

### 4. Volatility — Bollinger Bands

**Bollinger Middle Band: $42.32 (May 6)**

RRC's price ($41.17) is **trading below the middle band**, indicating bearish pressure. The distance from the middle band (~$1.15) is roughly 0.5 standard deviations (based on the ATR of ~1.43), meaning the price isn't at the extremes yet.

The Bollinger bands appear to be **moderately narrowing** (middle band descended from $44.34 to $42.32), which often precedes a significant price move. Combined with the **declining ATR** (from 1.71 in early April to 1.43 in early May), this signals **compressing volatility** — an explosive move (up or down) may be imminent.

### 5. Volatility — ATR (Average True Range)

**Current ATR: 1.43 (May 6)**

The ATR has declined from 1.71 (early April) to 1.43 (early May), a **16% decrease**, indicating **contracting volatility**. ATR levels below 1.50 are moderate for this stock. The declining ATR, combined with Bollinger Band compression, suggests the consolidation phase may be nearing its end — traders should prepare for a potential breakout.

### 6. Volume Analysis

A notable anomaly occurred on **April 22** with volume surging to **10.3 million shares** (nearly 3x the average), accompanied by a price jump from $41.67 to $43.25 (+3.8%). However, this spike was **not sustained** — volume returned to normal (~2–4M) and the price quickly retreated. This suggests the rally was driven partly by speculative buying rather than fundamental conviction.

Recent volume has been moderate (2–3.3M shares/day), indicating **lack of strong institutional interest** at these levels. A breakout above $43 on high volume would be a positive confirmation signal.

  3. MACD histogram turns positive, signaling bullish momentum resumption.

## Conclusion

RRC is experiencing a **healthy correction within a broader bullish trend**. The stock's ability to hold above the 200-SMA at $37.76 is reassuring for longer-term investors. However, the recent MACD bearish crossover, price below the 50-SMA, and declining RSI suggest **near-term caution**. The contracting volatility (declining ATR and narrowing Bollinger Bands) implies a significant move is likely coming soon — the direction remains uncertain.

**The optimal strategy is patience:** Wait for confirmation of bottoming via a bullish reversal pattern, followed by rising volume and improving momentum indicators, before committing to new positions. Existing holders should maintain their positions with disciplined stop-losses, as the long-term trend remains intact.
